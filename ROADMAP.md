# Roadmap

What is coming next, roughly in order. Plans change as each plugin is played with.

## Now
- **Lumen-Serra 1.1** is out: the saw synth, now with eight-note polyphony, and 36 presets. Bug reports and preset ideas welcome.
- **Lumen-Index 1.3** is out: smooth voice leading, next-chord hints, borrowed chords, and the catcher on the melody and bass guides. Bug reports welcome.
- **Lumen-Core:** shared parts for the whole suite. The tape engine and the design book (the look every window is built from) are in; knobs, meters and the preset code are next.

## Next
- **Lumen-Oxide:** the tape effect. The sound and presets are in testing; the plugin window is next.
- **Presets** across the suite: one save and load system, with factory presets for each plugin.

## Later
- **Lumen-Haze:** release.
- A single download with the whole suite.
